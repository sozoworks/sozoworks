# SOZOWORKS

Intent-driven systems for design, development, and AI collaboration.

---

## What this is

This repository group is a structured system for keeping products, design, AI usage, and implementation aligned to human intent and Core.

It is not a collection of isolated tools.

The system treats:

- Human intent
- Core definitions
- Documents as Source of Truth
- AI-assisted synchronization
- Product convergence
- Design systems
- Implementation constraints

as one connected structure.

---

## Core Philosophy

In this system:

- Human defines intent and makes decisions
- AI supports structuring, validation, and synchronization
- Documents become the source of truth
- Undefined information must not be guessed
- Contradictions must return upstream
- Why comes before expression

The goal is not to let AI decide.

The goal is to keep intent, structure, implementation, and outcomes aligned.

## Design Definition

Design is not decoration.

Design is the act of structuring intent, information, relationships, constraints, and experiences so that a purpose can be achieved consistently.

---

## AI Positioning

AI is not treated as:

- the decision maker
- the owner of intent
- the authority of truth

AI is treated as:

- a structuring system
- a synchronization assistant
- a validation layer
- a formatting and reasoning support system

Final responsibility always belongs to humans.
